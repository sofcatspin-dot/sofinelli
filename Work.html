Jetling Airlines

Jetling Airlines is the child company of Delta Airlines that serves exclusively unaccompanied minors. This airline provides a dedicated space for little passengers to feel safe and comfortable during their journey. Designed to be accessible to both children and their caretakers alike.

Mobile App

This interactive prototype demonstrates the core features and user flow of the Jetling Mobile App. Guardians can manage the children registered on their account in their account profile. Once their children are registered, parents can book their child's flight. When Designing the app, I wanted to create an interface parents could easily navigate. Although the airline is designed for children, the app is intended to be managed by guardians.

Logo and Mascot

It was important the visuals of the airline reflected the fun and playful nature of the brand. The logo is a simple paper plane icon, which is a universal symbol of flight and childhood. The mascot, Jet, is a friendly and adventurous lion cub inspired by Dusty the Delta Air Lion who was retired in the late nineties.

Widgets

These widgets are designed to provide quick access to important information and features of the Jetling Mobile App. These tools allow parents to easily view where their passanger is located at any given time.
Beidler Forest

Beidler Forest is a nature reserve located in South Carolina that's apart of the Audobon Society. This Merchandise campaign was designed to make bird watching funny and exciting. By including the birds commonly found in the forest and putting them on motorcycles this campaign brings a unique twist to the traditional bird watching experience.
